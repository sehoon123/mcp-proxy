kotlin-logging, Jansi, JetBrains annotations, and SLF4J. Exact runtime coordinates and SHA-256 hashes are generated in
`build/reports/runtime-components.txt` and embedded as `META-INF/independent-mcp-bridge/runtime-components.txt`.
